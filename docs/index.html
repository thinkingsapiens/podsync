A lean, native macOS podcast client

Built for Apple Silicon with Tauri. Syncs your subscriptions and play state with gpodder.net, works fully offline, and skips ads using an on-device model — nothing ever leaves your Mac.

Download for Mac Read the docs

macOS 11 Big Sur or later · Apple Silicon (M1 and newer)

Offline-first

SQLite is the source of truth on your Mac. gpodder.net is a sync target, not a dependency — the app works fully offline.

On-device ad detection

Flags and can auto-skip ads using a small local model. Your transcripts and listening data never leave your machine.

gpodder.net sync

Subscriptions and play state stay in sync across every gpodder.net-connected client you use, resumable after any outage.

Menu bar mini player

Close the window and keep listening from a compact tray player, without the app taking up Dock space.

Built for Apple Silicon

A native Tauri shell, not an Electron wrapper — small install size and low idle resource use on M-series Macs.

Self-updating

PodSync checks for new versions and can update itself in place, so you're never stuck manually tracking releases.
